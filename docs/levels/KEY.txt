LEVEL SHEETS: THE KEY

Every level is a grid of cells, two characters per cell, one space between cells.
The FIRST character says what the cell is. The SECOND is its one parameter: a group,
an angel, a level or a facing. If the thing has no parameter, the first character
is repeated. Each cell holds exactly one glyph. A floor cell holds at most one thing.

The sheets in this folder are written by `node tools/levelsheet.mjs` from the build's
own level data, so they always match the game. Don't edit them by hand; change the level
in src/build-iray.mjs and re-run the tool.


TERRAIN

  []   solid wall
  ..   open floor
  |n   removable wall in group n (1-9). It is solid until group n opens.
  |+   removable wall in MORE THAN ONE group: whichever opens first takes it out.
       The sheet lists its groups under the map.


ON THE FLOOR (each stands on an open cell)

  P>   player start, facing east     P<   facing west
  Pv   facing south                  P^   facing north
       This is the nearest of the four. The exact pose is under the map.
  An   angel number n (1-9, in the order the build places them)
  Ln   leash cell for angel n: angel n may never enter it
  OO   orb
  II   idol, the errand rooms' prize, carried back to the hub's pedestals
  Sn   socket: seating an orb here opens wall group n
  /n   pedestal: seating an idol here helps open group n. A group held by
       pedestals opens only when ALL of them are fed; sockets have no such gate.
  Xn   exit hole, dropping you into level n
  X>   exit hole to the next level in the chain
  X!   exit hole to THE END, the ending sequence


GRID

  Columns are numbered across the top, with the tens digit on its own line above the
  units digit. Rows are numbered down the left. x runs east and y runs south, so the
  cell (x, y) is column x, row y. Positions in the notes are (x, y). Entities stand at
  the CENTRE of a cell: an orb at (6,5) is at 6.5, 5.5.

  The map is at most 256 cells (W x H <= 256, and in practice <= 253). The outer
  ring is solid wall.


UNDER THE MAP

  size     W x H and the cell count
  start    exact position and heading (0 = east, 90 = south), plus the PX/PY/PA/PAF
           values to start a build there
  inks     the three colours a room is made of: ground (ceiling and floor, $d021),
           walls ($d022), seams ($d023). C64 colour number, then name.
  flicker  the failing-light inks, shown for single frames (only on levels that have it)
  floor    dithered: the floor half is darkened with a black checker
  walls    marked: a dot in every solid wall cell
  exit     each exit: where it leads, and the colour of the hole
  group n  the cells of wall group n, and which sockets or pedestals open it
  |+       which groups a shared wall cell belongs to
  leash    which cells each leashed angel may not enter
  idol     present on the levels that hold one
